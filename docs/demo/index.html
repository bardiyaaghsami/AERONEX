Demo video

59 seconds of recorded project footage, with no audio. It shows the simulated warehouse, the vehicle and its simulated sensors, LiDAR perception, the RTAB-Map reconstruction and the benchmark results.

Chapters

What the footage is

Simulator recordingsGazebo Harmonic, RViz and ROS 2 topic recordings from the project. Nothing is generated or interpolated.
Flight testThe take-off is the PX4 SITL flight test in the open test world, not a flight in the warehouse.
3D reconstructionThe orbit is a virtual camera moving around the real exported RTAB-Map point cloud. The orange path is the offline optimised trajectory, and it is followed by the original RViz screen recording of the same map.
ResultsOnline odometry, offline optimisation and observed-map completeness are shown separately, as in the report.
